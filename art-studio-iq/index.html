What it does

  • Pricing and profit math

    Enter your hours, materials, overhead and where you sell. Get a price that covers all of it.

  • What you keep after fees

    Compare marketplaces, galleries, pop-ups and direct sales side by side and see what you keep from each sale.

  • Studio Eye

    Look at your work the way you do in the studio: value view, notan, composition grids, mirror and squint. The tools show. You judge.

  • Listing photo prep

    Straighten, crop and color-correct a phone photo, add your signature mark, and export it at marketplace sizes with location data removed.

  • The writing desk

    Your own questions, templates and saved snippets, with character counts for each place you sell. Every word is yours.

  • Inventory and provenance

    Keep every piece, where it is, who has it and its sales history in one catalog.

  • Supplies with reorder alerts

    Track paint, surfaces and packaging, and get a reminder when something runs low.

  • Licensing and usage rights

    Record every deal that lets someone use your work, with dates, territory and exclusivity, and get a warning when two deals conflict.

  • Simple offline lists

    Inventory, price lists and packing checklists in plain, large type that work without a connection.

  • Sales insights

    See what sells, where and how fast, in plain sentences based on your own records.

  • Commissions

    Track deposits, milestones and revision limits so projects stay on scope.

  • Business dashboards

    Charts you can arrange and tap into, with a table for every chart.

No AI inside

Art Studio IQ has no AI. It never writes for you, rates your work or sends your records anywhere. The tools show you your numbers and your work, and you decide.

Made to be easy to use

Choose your font, spacing and background color. Turn off motion. Switch on a focus mode that shows one step at a time, or have text read aloud. The app is designed for people with dyslexia and ADHD, and for use with a screen reader.
