## Art Studio IQ page

- **Page title:** Art Studio IQ: price your work and track your studio | Void Bloom Art Studio
- **Page description:** An Android app for working artists: price your work, see what you keep after fees, and track pieces, supplies and certificates. No AI. Pay once.
- **Heading:** Know what to charge and what you keep.
- **Intro:** Art Studio IQ is an app for working artists. It prices your pieces, tracks your inventory and supplies, and shows what you keep after fees. Your records stay on your phone.
- **Android and iPhone:** It starts on Android. On iPhone? Tell us when you join the list.
- **Features heading:** What it does

**Features (title, then text):**
- **Pricing and profit math.** Enter your hours, materials, overhead and where you sell. Get a price that covers all of it.
- **What you keep after fees.** Compare marketplaces, galleries, pop-ups and direct sales side by side and see what you keep from each sale.
- **Studio Eye.** Look at your work the way you do in the studio: value view, notan, composition grids, mirror and squint. The tools show. You judge.
- **Listing photo prep.** Straighten, crop and color-correct a phone photo, add your signature mark, and export it at marketplace sizes with location data removed.
- **The writing desk.** Your own questions, templates and saved snippets, with character counts for each place you sell. Every word is yours.
- **Inventory and provenance.** Keep every piece, where it is, who has it and its sales history in one catalog.
- **Supplies with reorder alerts.** Track paint, surfaces and packaging, and get a reminder when something runs low.
- **Licensing and usage rights.** Record every deal that lets someone use your work, with dates, territory and exclusivity, and get a warning when two deals conflict.
- **Simple offline lists.** Inventory, price lists and packing checklists in plain, large type that work without a connection.
- **Sales insights.** See what sells, where and how fast, in plain sentences based on your own records.
- **Commissions.** Track deposits, milestones and revision limits so projects stay on scope.
- **Business dashboards.** Charts you can arrange and tap into, with a table for every chart.

- **No AI inside, heading:** No AI inside
- **No AI inside, statement:** Art Studio IQ has no AI. It never writes for you, rates your work or sends your records anywhere. The tools show you your numbers and your work, and you decide.
- **Easy to use, heading:** Made to be easy to use
- **Easy to use, text:** Choose your font, spacing and background color. Turn off motion. Switch on a focus mode that shows one step at a time, or have text read aloud. The app is designed for people with dyslexia and ADHD, and for use with a screen reader.
- **Tiers heading:** Start free. Pay once to unlock more.

**Tiers (no prices):**
- **Free.** Pricing calculator, net-after-fees comparison, simple lists, Studio Eye, photo prep and the writing desk, with room for 50 pieces.
- **Essentials.** Everything in Free, with unlimited pieces, certificates, supplies and license records, plus commissions and sales insights. Pay once.
- **Pro.** Dashboard customization and 3D dashboards, for people who own Essentials. Pay once.

- **Closing:** Join the list and we'll tell you when it's ready.
