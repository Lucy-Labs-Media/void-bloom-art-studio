# Art Studio IQ site copy: Lindsey's words

Write every section below in your own words, then tell Jordan. He places your text on the site exactly as you wrote it.

Rules: no AI writing tools (a plain spell-checker is fine). No prices, ratings, download counts, awards or release dates. Say only what the app can do today. Don't write "certified" or "compliant" about accessibility.

Each section lists what it has to do and where it shows up. Those lines are a brief, not wording.

---

## Home section (the "Coming soon" block on the home page)

**Headline.** Names who the app is for or the one result they get. Shown big. Home page only.

**Promise.** One sentence under the headline. It should answer "what does this do for me?"

**Benefit 1, Benefit 2, Benefit 3.** Three short lines. Pick the three things you most wish you'd had as a working artist.

**No AI line.** One sentence. It must match your About statement.

**Form heading.** Sits above the email box.

**Form line.** What the person gets by joining. Be specific about what arrives and when it arrives (without a date).

**Reassurance.** One line under the button about what you will and won't do with their email.

**Thank you heading.**

**Thank you, Android.** Shown to people who pick Android. Tell them what happens next.

**Thank you, iPhone.** Shown to people who pick iPhone. Tell them honestly where iPhone stands. (Jordan counts these answers to decide whether to build an iPhone version, so be straight with them.)

**Thank you, both.** Shown to people who pick Both.

**Thank you, general.** Shown if someone returns from the no-JavaScript path.
